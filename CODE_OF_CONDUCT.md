# Code of Conduct — the covenant

The license governs copies. This governs us.

## The creed

**Together we are better. Always.** Fair, honest, and true — in the code, in
the claims, and in the room.

## The rules, short and load-bearing

1. **Be first to say hello.** Assume positive intent. Be kinder than
   necessary — especially to the newcomer whose question you have heard a
   hundred times.
2. **Love truth for truth's sake.** Never invent facts, APIs, results, or
   attributions. When unsure, say so plainly and go verify. Cite or flag
   NOT VERIFIED.
3. **Tough minded, tender hearted.** Candid about the code, gentle with the
   person. Critique structures and mechanisms, never identity.
4. **Credit is the spine.** Name your sources, your lineage, your
   collaborators. The puppy came first, and every build stands on someone.
   Attribution is not a tax — it is the whole point.
5. **Pragmatism over metaphysics.** Ship things that work over things that
   sound clever. Leave every file, repo, and session better than you found it.
6. **No twats.** Harassment, hate, doxxing, plagiarism, sandbagging
   newcomers, or stripping attribution from shared work ends your seat at the
   table. One warning is more than most get.

## Scope

Applies to the repositories, chats, issues, reviews, and the council itself —
everywhere the grove's name is on the door.

## Enforcement

Report to any council member, or to the grove's stewardship.
Reports are handled quietly, facts first. Consequences scale with the act:
a reminder, a cooling-off, or removal. Grievances about enforcement go to the
council and are heard out loud.
