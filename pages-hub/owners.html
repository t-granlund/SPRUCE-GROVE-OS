A spoken sentence became a working lead pipeline

Benton Drones — a drone outfit that needed every lead caught, routed, and tracked without anyone babysitting a form.

  • The brief was spoken, not written. Goals recorded as voice, transcribed on-device, turned into a working charter — the club's first question asked of the work itself.
  • They studied the brand before writing a line. The machine surveyed the company's live storefront — typeface, palette, even how the site loads — and the intake page was built to look like it belonged.
  • The machine built; the human gated. Every step that touched the live site passed through human review first. The handoff notes are so careful they warn about the typo a person might make.
  • Tests before trust. Automated checks drove the form the way a customer does — before launch, not after complaints.
  • Live, end to end, verified. Domain, certificate, then the whole pipeline proven working: form → geocode → ticket → dashboard
  • It stayed maintained. Follow-up voice notes became changelog entries. Maintenance is not a hostage situation.

The way of working behind it

the pledge

Where every dollar goes — 43 Institute

The machine stays free. The value flows to the misfits. The ledger stays public.

  • The commitment. Any profit the grove's work ever makes — videos, sponsorships, anything — is ledgered publicly and donated to the 43 Institute (43i.org), Ken Block's foundation: a 501(c)(3) creating pathways and resources for underserved, highly driven "motivated misfits" in action sports, motorsports, and the creative arts.
  • Why 43i. Racing cars and rally, skateboards and BMX, art and music and print — kids who love things loudly and lack the means to chase them. Their pillars are mentorship pathways, access, skills, and health; their own story is a kid in a screen-printing class whose art project became DC Shoes.
  • The connection. We recognize the species. The grove's proof is a woman with no college degree who became a director of the work at thirty-five — a motivated misfit handed a pathway. The Method is a mentorship pathway. The club is a mentorship institution. Same math: talent is evenly distributed; means are not.
  • The transparency. Donations to 43i are unrestricted and used where needed most (their words); our ledger of what was earned and given will be published beside the work. No quiet skimming, ever.
