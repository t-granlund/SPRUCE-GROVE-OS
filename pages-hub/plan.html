I · The instance, tuned to your means

choose the engine — the harness makes every one of them go further

Spruce Grove runs on the AI you choose: Synthetic.new, Claude, ChatGPT, or your own setup. You bring the subscription you can afford; the harness stretches it — transparent usage you can watch as it lands, session history that survives anything, context you can steer mid-run, and model routing that spends cheap tokens on small work and saves the strong ones for the hard parts. One command sets it up; one command checks it any time.

III · The lifecycle — provisioned by who you are

identity as the key: access, tools, and software that follow your role and need — from a crew of one to the biggest of big

Provisioning on arrival

A new person, role, or need appears — and the access, tools, and accounts that role requires appear with it. Dynamically, by identity and need, with every grant recorded.

Deprovisioning on exit

The inverse, done properly and immediately: keys rotated, access removed, records kept. The step every enterprise knows is the dangerous one — automated, logged, boring.

Domains, software, users — one ledger

The same lifecycle discipline for the whole estate: who owns which domain, which software instance, which account. Renewals, expirations, and handoffs as workflows, not as 3 a.m. surprises.

Security as provenance

Identity-as-a-service the grove way: the same receipt culture that keeps the code honest keeps the access honest. Every grant, every revocation, every change — attributable, reviewable, and reversible.

This is the scale promise, stated plainly: the smallest of small gets the same machinery as the biggest of big — because it is the same machine, sized by its plugins and its keys, never by its price tag. The shop on the square and the enterprise across the country run the same grove; what changes is how much of the stack they switch on.
