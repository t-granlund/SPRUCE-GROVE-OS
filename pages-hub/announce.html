Spruce Grove OS · v1.0.0 · “the grove speaks”

The agentic coding CLI
that speaks your language.

Say what you need out loud. Spruce Grove transcribes it on-device with a local whisper model, shows you the words, lets you tune them, and hands them to the agent working in your repo. Open source. Your voice never leaves your Mac.

voice-first

Think out loud. Ship anyway.

The whole loop is local until you choose to send: capture, transcription, review. The agent only ever sees words you approved.

01 · SPEAK

Record

Hit /rec in the CLI or the mic button in the desktop app. Pause and resume — riffs welcome.

02 · LISTEN BACK

Transcribe locally

whisper-large-v3-turbo runs on your Metal GPU. No cloud, no queue, no telemetry.

03 · TUNE

Review & edit

The transcript lands in an editable prompt. Re-record against your own draft until it sings.

04 · SEND

Dispatch the agent

Your approved words become the prompt, applied against the directory you're working in.

PLUGINS

Three tiers, one golden rule

Builtin, user, and project plugins with a SHA-256 trust ceremony. Every feature in 1.0 shipped without touching core.

Sub-agents, skills, model routing — inherited from the Code Puppy harness, rebranded to the grove and kept compatible upstream.

Local by default

Transcription is on-device. Provider credentials live in your macOS keychain, referenced by rotation-safe env-refs, never in plaintext configs.
