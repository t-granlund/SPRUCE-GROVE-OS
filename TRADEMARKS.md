# Trademarks

The Apache-2.0 license on this repository governs the *code*. It does not
grant rights to the names and marks. This page states plainly what belongs
to whom, so nobody has to guess.

## The Grove's marks

- **Spruce Grove** and **Spruce Grove OS** — the project and product name
- **The TreeMark** — the three-spruce mark (center tree, two flanking, one
  ground line), in any of its canonical forms
- **The grove badge** — the encircled badge form of the TreeMark
- **"Together we are better. Always."** — the creed, as a mark of the project

These identify the origin of the software. You may use them to say, truthfully,
that your work is built with or derived from Spruce Grove OS (nominative use —
that is fair and encouraged). You may not use them to name, brand, or endorse
a product or service as if it were the Grove's own, or in any way that
suggests the Grove endorses you. A NOTICE-file mention describing origin is
always welcome — that is the license working as designed.
